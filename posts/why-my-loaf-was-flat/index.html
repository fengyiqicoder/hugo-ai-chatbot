Why my loaf came out flat (and how I fixed it)

November 2, 2026 · 1 min read

Three flat loaves in a row in October. Here is what it was, in the order I checked.

The starter was not strong enough

A starter that does not double within 6 hours of a feed is too weak to raise a loaf. Mine had slowed down because the kitchen got colder. Two feeds a day for three days, kept somewhere warm (the oven with only the light on), brought it back.

Bulk fermentation was too short

The clock is not the test; the dough is. At 20 °C bulk took 11 hours, not 9. Wait until the dough has grown by about half, has bubbles on the sides, and wobbles when you shake the bowl.

Too much water for the flour

My new flour absorbs less water. Going from 75% to 70% hydration (350 g of water for 500 g of flour) gave a dough I could actually shape.

Shaping without tension

A loose shape spreads in the oven. Pull the dough towards you on an unfloured counter until the surface is tight, then let it rest 10 minutes and do it once more.
