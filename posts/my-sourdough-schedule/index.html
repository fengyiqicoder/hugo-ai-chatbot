My weekday sourdough schedule

October 18, 2026 · 1 min read

I work office hours, so my bread has to fit around them. This is the schedule I have used for a year, for one loaf with 500 g of flour.

The night before

At 10 pm I feed the starter: 20 g starter, 50 g flour, 50 g water. It sits on the counter overnight at about 22 °C.

Day one

Time Step
7:00 am Mix 500 g flour with 350 g water and leave it (autolyse)
7:45 am Add 100 g starter and 10 g salt, squeeze it in
8:00 am One set of stretch and folds, then off to work
6:00 pm Bulk is done when the dough has grown about 50% and jiggles
6:30 pm Shape, put it in a floured banneton, cover, into the fridge

Day two

Straight from the fridge, any time between 7 am and 7 pm. Bake in a Dutch oven at 250 °C: 20 minutes with the lid on, then 25 minutes at 230 °C with the lid off.

The long cold proof is what makes this work: anywhere from 12 to 24 hours in the fridge is fine, and longer gives a more sour loaf.
