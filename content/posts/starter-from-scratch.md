---
title: "Making a sourdough starter from scratch"
date: 2026-09-20
---

All you need is flour, water and a jar. Rye or whole wheat flour gets going faster than white flour.

## Days 1 to 3

Mix 50 g whole wheat flour with 50 g water in a jar, cover loosely, and leave it at room temperature. Every 24 hours, throw away all but 50 g and feed it 50 g flour and 50 g water.

## Days 4 to 7

Switch to feeding every 12 hours. Bubbles on day 2 that vanish on day 4 are normal: the first bacteria die off before the yeast takes over.

## When is it ready?

When it reliably doubles within 4 to 6 hours of a feed and smells pleasantly sour, usually between day 7 and day 14. Then keep it in the fridge and feed it once a week.
